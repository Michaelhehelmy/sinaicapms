### 4.3 Frozen-history pointers — do **not** rewrite

Three code pointers cite documents this plan archives, and they must be **left as-is**:
`backend/migrations/0127_meals_tenant_composite_pk.sql:36-37`,
`backend/migrations/0115_rooms_new_tenant_not_null_fk.sql`, and
`backend/src/routes/registry.js:1774`-adjacent migration comments. A migration's rationale
comment is a record of what was known when the migration was written; editing it to point at a
doc that has since been reorganised falsifies the history it exists to preserve. **Recommendation:
leave frozen-migration citations pointing at the archived path** (paths change, files don't) and
record the archive path in `docs/archive/README.md`. Only *test* comments and *live source*
comments should be repointed — and even then, to the archive path is acceptable if the section
anchor still resolves.
